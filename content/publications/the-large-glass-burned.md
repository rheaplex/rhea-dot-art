**The Large Glass, Burned, 2018**

Decades from now, in a world in which physical art has long since been forgotten in favour of NFTs, an art collector believes that destroying one particular token with a curious past will make them the greatest artist who has ever lived.

“is this the defining creative act, or the greatest art crime, of our age?”
