**V[R]erse 12: “Bridall Day”, 2021, 3D Sculpture by Mez Breeze with text by Rhea Myers**

Mez Breeze invited me to provide a short text to use as part of her [“V[R]erses” XR story series](https://mezbreezedesign.com/vr-literature/vrerses-xr-story-series/).

The result is a densely encrypted nanohyperfiction self-portrait.
