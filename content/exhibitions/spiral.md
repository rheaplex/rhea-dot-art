A group exhibition of eleven artists who helped shape the languages of AI, generative and protocol-based art, revisiting formative tools, methods and unresolved ideas through new digital and physical works. Presented by SuperRare during NFT NYC, with an online release on [SuperRare](https://superrare.com/) from 4 September.

With Anne Spalter, Robbie Barrat, Pindar Van Arman, Kyle McDonald, Bård Ionson, Atay Ilgun, artonymousartifakt, Gene Kogan, Mario Klingemann and Helena Sarin.
