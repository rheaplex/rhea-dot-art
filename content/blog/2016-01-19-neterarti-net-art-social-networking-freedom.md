[Neterarti](https://neterarti.furtherfield.org/) is [Furtherfield](http://furtherfield.org/)'s new social network for net artists based on the [GNU social](http://gnu.io/social/) Free Software social network system. If you're familiar with Twitter it's very similar, and it's easy to access via the web or desktop and mobile apps.

Sign up and start netting and arting!
