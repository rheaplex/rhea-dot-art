I was invited to show something at the 2018 [Gray Area Festival](http://grayareafestival.io/), a great Art &amp; Technology event in San Francisco. This year it was on from July 26-29, 2018. I took the opportunity to make something new.

The result was SchellingFlags.

![](/images/2018/09/schellingflags-300x170.png)

SchellingFlags is the meme of “…but on the blockchain” applied to flags, with a touch of Art &amp; Language and Komar &amp; Melamid. You can find out more about it [on the project page](/schellingflags/). The version shown is from during development, so the last two flags haven’t been voted for and thereby defined yet.

Massive thanks to Gray Area for putting on an awesome event and making my work part of it!
