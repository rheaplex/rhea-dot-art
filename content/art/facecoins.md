_A blockchain portrait that starts with you._

Facecoins is a tokenized edition of the classic [Facecoin](/facecoin/) (2014) with a twist. The genesis block and the colours of the proof-of-work bitmaps for each edition are derived from the Ethereum wallet address of its owner. This makes each edition a kind of portrait, or search for a portrait, of the owner, or of their blockchain identity.
