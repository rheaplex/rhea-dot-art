From the _Portents_ whitepaper:

> We propose a scheme for encoding references to Ordinals in Bitcoin block hashes as they are mined.
> We then demonstrate how to search the existing Bitcoin blockchain for such references.
> Since any references that are found predate the possibility of their existence, their existence would function as proof of future events.
> This is prophecy, which is to say time travel.
> Having established the import of the discovery of these portents, we explain strategies that we may pursue to make ourselves worthy of them.
