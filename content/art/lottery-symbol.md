**Lottery Symbol, 2017, Ethereum DApp**

A graphical symbol on the blockchain, chosen from options submitted via Ethereum transactions in a simple lottery.

The lottery uses the blockchain itself as a source of entropy, which is known to be insecure but this is just a symbol rather than millions of dollars.

This is a piece in a series of works that pair methods of allocation with aesthetic properties. It follows on from [Democratic Palette](democratic-palette).
