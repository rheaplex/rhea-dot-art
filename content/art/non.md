**_Non_ (2025), Service Contract Offer and NFTs**

_Non_ continues from the fiat state law forms of _Certificate of
Inauthenticity_, the selective concealment of _Secret Artwork_, and
the non-property of _Tokens Equal Text_. Its NFTs disavow their status
as art and property in favor of a stranger arrangement with their
viewer and owner. Agreement grants access to the content of the
artwork - or may be the content of the artwork.

The anxieties of late startup culture and the mispriced fears and
entitlements of the young men it exploits are reflected in the
proliferation of Non-Disparagement, Non-Disclosure, and Non-Compete
clauses with perpetual and cascading extents within tech industry
employment contracts. Love, honour, and obey. These fears find their
symbolic resolution in blockchain consent registration system
proposals but have real effects in society offchain.
