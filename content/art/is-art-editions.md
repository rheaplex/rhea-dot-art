**Is Art (Token) (2023) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

*Own the creative power of a crypto artist.*

Is Art (Token) takes the artist’s power of nomination, of naming something as art, and delegates it to the artwork’s owner. Their assertion of its art or non-art status is secured and guaranteed by the power of the blockchain. Based on a common and understandable misunderstanding of how [Is Art](/is-art/) (2014) works, this is the first in a series of editions that inscribe ongoing and contemporary concerns onto this exemplar of a past or perhaps not yet realized blockchain artworld.

**Is Art (Token, Block Height) (2023) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that is art (or not) depending on the current Ethereum block height.

**Is Art (Token, Because) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art for a particular reason by its owner.

**Is Art (Token, Burn) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art by its owner, but only by destroying it.

**Is Art (Token, Composition) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that has several sub-tokens attached that can be arranged to nominate it as art by its owner, possibly in co-operation with other owners.

**Is Art (Token, Democratic) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art (or not) by a simple majority of its owners.

**Is Art (Token, Democratic Palette) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner and that displays this state using colours from Democratic Palette (2016).

**Is Art (Token, Griefing) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art by any of its owners, overriding the vote of all the others.

**Is Art (Token, Is X) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art by its owner for reasons that they specify using a controlled vocabulary.

**Is Art (Token, Lottery) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner if they are lucky.

**Is Art (Token, Nomination) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be used to nominate another token as art by its owner.

**Is Art (Token, Proof-of-Work) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner on completion of a proof-of-work puzzle.

**Is Art (Token, Proxy) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner and that stores uts state for everyone in the original Is Art (2014/2015).

**Is Art (Token, Secret) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token that can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner in a way that is encrypted and known only to them.

**Is Art (Token Metadata) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

A token with metadata that can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner.

**Is Art (This) (2024) Ethereum ERC-721 Token in edition of 16.**

This can be nominated as art (or not) by its owner.
